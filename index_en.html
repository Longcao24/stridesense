🚶 Walk mode · foot-mounted PDR
Strap the phone to the top of your foot or ankle (screen facing out, snug, no wobble). Hold still and tap "② Calibrate", then tap "▶ Start walk tracking" above and walk normally.
How it works: the instant each step lands and is still, velocity must be 0 → an automatic ZUPT wipes the drift built up over that step, so you can walk far without drifting. The right (top-down) view is the ground path you walked; it also counts steps and distance.

📖 How to use & how it works
Rotation / orientation: the left view follows the phone orientation in real time, almost no drift → reliable.

In-air drawing: the right view traces the path your hand sweeps through the air. Steps:
1. Lay the phone flat and still → tap "Calibrate gravity" (~1.5 s, do not move).
2. Tap "Start recording".
3. Pick up the phone and draw a circle / write a character in the air (within 2–4 s).
4. Tap "Stop recording" — the right view shows the path; "Replay" animates it; "Export" saves the data for Python analysis.

Physical limits (honest note): position = double integral of acceleration, so error grows with time squared and inevitably drifts beyond ~5–10 s. This tool uses high-pass bias removal + zero-velocity update (ZUPT) + velocity leak to keep short gestures shape-recognizable, but it is not physically accurate at centimeter scale. For centimeter accuracy, add visual / magnetic / RF aiding.
